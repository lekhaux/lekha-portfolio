About Me

Designing for the moments
that matter in finance

My name is Lekha Mer. I'm a product designer at the beginning of my journey — but with a very clear direction and a strong foundation built through real research work.

I focus exclusively on fintech because financial products carry real stakes. A confusing interface costs someone their savings. A missing risk signal costs someone their trust. Bad UX in finance isn't just frustrating — it's harmful.

"Great fintech design makes the right decision feel obvious at the right moment."

My background combines a BBA in Marketing and Finance (Saurashtra University, 75%) with a PGCM in Digital Marketing (Symbiosis, A+ grade) — giving me both the business context and the user empathy to design products that work for real people.

Through simulations with Lloyds Banking Group, BCG X and bp × Jio, I've practiced the full research-to-design cycle. I'm actively looking for junior roles and internships in fintech product design.
